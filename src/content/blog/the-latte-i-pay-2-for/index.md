Most mornings, I'm up at 3:30. With twin boys at home, if I want any time to myself, it has to be before everyone else wakes up. So the early hours are mine: working on my startups, some meditation, a walk.

And a coffee. A large oat latte from my local Caffè Nero, first thing. It's about £4.50 a day, and it adds up: over a year, that's more than £1,600.

So I found a way to pay £1 for it instead. It's £2 now, but I'll get to that.

![Three business sim packs from Three, laid out on a carpet above four pocket Wi-Fi hotspots: two Vodafone and two ZTE](./sims-and-hotspots.jpg)

## Three+

Three, the mobile network, has a rewards programme. It's had a few different names over the years, and now it's called Three+. If you're a Three customer, you're in it.

One of the rewards is a weekly Caffè Nero drink. It used to be £1. A couple of months ago it went up to £2. The terms on the reward screen are pretty generous:

- 1 code per customer per week
- any barista-made hot or cold drink, any size
- milk alternatives and extras included
- 1 hour to use it once you've activated it

![The Three+ "Your reward code" screen, with the QR code blurred and the conditions underneath: any barista-made hot or cold drink for £2, one code per customer per week](./reward-code.jpg)

## Why one sim isn't enough

The catch is that it's one reward a week. I drink coffee every day.

More Three sims would mean more rewards, but a normal sim means a contract, and even a cheap one is around £10 a month. For six extra sims, that's £60 a month just to unlock six coffees a week. Suddenly the cheap coffee isn't cheap at all.

## The trick

Then I found out about Three business data sims, sold through a reseller. You pay £25 once and get 10GB of data a month for two years. No top-ups, nothing else to pay.

Each one comes with its own number, and each number can be registered with Three+. So I bought seven. Seven sims, seven coffees a week.

Offers like this change, so if you fancy trying it, check what's on offer now rather than taking my prices as gospel.

## The honest bit

It's a loophole, and you do have to go out of your way for it. Here's what it takes:

- **Cheap phones.** £8 4G phones, so each sim can receive its registration text.
- **Old pocket hotspots.** These hold the sims.
- **Clone apps.** You need the Three+ app open to show the QR code, so I use apps like Parallel Space to run several copies of it on one phone.

![A phone folder called Utilities with Parallel Space, Parallel App, 2Accounts, LocalSend and a second Parallel Space icon](./clone-apps.jpg)

It's fiddly, I won't pretend otherwise. But I've done it long enough that I've got it down to a routine.

## The money

Seven sims at £25 each is £175, and that covers two years. My first set ran out, so I bought a second. That's £350 so far.

When the coffee was £1, I was saving about £3.50 a cup. At £2, it's about £2.50. Over the last few years, that's added up to thousands of pounds.

## Still a bargain

The price going up to £2 stung a little. But a large oat latte for £2, every morning, is still a bargain.
